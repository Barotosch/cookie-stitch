# Cookie Stitch branding

The Cookie Stitch name and the app logo in `public/cookie-icon-*.png` are excluded from the repository's MIT grant. You can use the name to credit Cookie Stitch or describe where a fork came from. When distributing a fork as an app, give it its own name and logo so people can tell which app they are using.

The [First Cookie demo pattern](examples/first-cookie.cookie-stitch.json), including its embedded pattern images, is covered by the [MIT License](LICENSE). You can keep, change or share it with a fork.
